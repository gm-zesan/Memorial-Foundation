Md. Mashiur Rahman

2 October 1951 – 28 June 2022

Freedom Fighter & Philanthropist

Founder's Inspiration

A Life Defined by Quiet Kindness

“In the quietest acts of kindness lies the greatest light.”

Freedom Fighter Md. Mashiur Rahman was an individual whose life was guided not by material wealth or public acclaim, but by humility, steadfast faith, and an unconditional commitment to serving humanity.

Established by his loving family, the Foundation carries forward his noble ethos as ongoing charity (Sadaqah Jariyah)—ensuring that vulnerable families, hungry children, and neglected communities continue to receive nourishment, shelter, and educational opportunity in his honor.

Silent Charity

Extending help with pure intentions, bringing peace through giving without seeking recognition.

1971 Freedom Fighter

Valiant patriotism translated into lifelong civic duty, treating everyone with deep dignity.

Sadaqah Jariyah

Enduring charity delivering sustainable water, education, and meals to communities in need.
