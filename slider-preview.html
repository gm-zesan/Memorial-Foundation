⚡ 1. Diagonal Shutter Wipe (Clip-Path)

How it works: Rather than a cheap cross-fade where images wash out into each other, the next image slices in diagonally using clip-path: polygon(...).

Why it looks premium: It looks like a high-end camera shutter or editorial magazine page turning. Used extensively by luxury brands on Awwwards.

⚡ 2. Kinetic Parallax Mask (Counter-Directional)

How it works: The container slides right-to-left, while the image inside moves left-to-right at a slower speed.

Why it looks premium: Creates hypnotic 3D depth. It stops the slider from feeling like a generic PowerPoint presentation and makes it feel like an agency showcase.

⚡ 3. Circular Iris Aperture (Camera Lens)

How it works: The new photo expands from an organic circle or aperture mask from the center outwards to 100% full screen.

Why it looks premium: Evokes documentary film storytelling. Gives emotional weight to each slide change.

⚡ 4. Editorial Typography Line-Mask

How it works: Instead of the whole headline fading up and down with blur, the words slide up from an invisible slit (overflow: hidden mask).

Why it looks premium: The text snaps into place with crisp authority. Zero lag, zero blur, completely sharp and dignified.
