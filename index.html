About Mashiur Rahman

Freedom Fighter
Md. Mashiur Rahman

“A Life Rich in Humanity, Not in Wealth”

“Some people leave this world, but their kindness, values, and compassion remain in the hearts of others. Their lives may come to an end, but the inspiration created by their good deeds lives on.”

Freedom Fighter Md. Mashiur Rahman was one such individual, a man whose life was defined not by material wealth or social status, but by integrity, faith, patriotism, forgiveness, and an unwavering commitment to humanity.

He lived a modest life with limited financial resources. Yet, within those limitations, he possessed an extraordinarily generous heart. Whatever he had, he shared with others—extending a helping hand to people in need and contributing to the well-being of his family, neighbors, friends, and community. For him, helping others was never about recognition—it was simply a source of happiness, fulfillment, and inner peace.

Selfless Devotion & Feeding Others

He particularly loved feeding people and welcoming guests. Offering a warm meal or comforting someone in distress gave him profound inner peace.

Faith, Integrity & Responsibility

A devout Muslim whose faith was reflected through humility and honesty. He passed away without leaving any outstanding personal debts.

Spirit of Forgiveness

Even when wronged, he chose unconditional forgiveness. People recall that he had no enemies, never spoke harshly, and treated all with dignity.

Patriotic Freedom Fighter

As a valiant Freedom Fighter in 1971, his patriotism continued through lifelong compassion, civic duty, and continuous service to people.

Freedom Fighter Md. Mashiur Rahman

2 October 1951 – 28 June 2022

The Foundation's Mission

In Islamic belief, ongoing charity (Sadaqah Jariyah) continues to bring spiritual rewards even after passing. The family established the Foundation to continue his principles—supporting orphan care, clean water, warm meals, and humanitarian assistance.

Tejgaon Shishu Poribar Memorial Lunch: In loving remembrance of his birthday on 2 October 2026, a special feast was arranged for approximately 200 children at Tejgaon Shishu Poribar in Dhaka.

“May Almighty Allah forgive his shortcomings, accept his good deeds, grant him Jannatul Ferdous, and accept all humanitarian initiatives in his memory as Sadaqah Jariyah. Ameen.”
