Annual Memorial & Dua Mahfil

A solemn annual gathering honoring our founder late Mashiur Rahman's enduring legacy. Rural elders, community youth, and family members join in Quran recitation, heartfelt supplications (Dua), and a communal feast for over 500 village neighbors.
