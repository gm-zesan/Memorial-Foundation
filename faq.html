What is the mission of the Mashiur Rahman Memorial Foundation?

The foundation was established to honor the life, empathy, and enduring humanitarian service of late Mashiur Rahman. Our mission is to carry forward his vision by providing emergency winter warmth, deep tube-wells for clean drinking water, full educational sponsorships for orphans, and sustainable relief to impoverished communities across Bangladesh.

How is my donation utilized, and how is financial transparency guaranteed?

We uphold a strict 100% grassroots aid delivery policy. Core administrative expenses are covered by the founding family and dedicated trust endowment, ensuring that public donations go directly to beneficiaries on the ground. We provide transparent digital reports, verified field photographs, and an annual public audit available on our Transparency page.

How can donors in Bangladesh contribute via bKash, Nagad, or Bank Transfer?

Donors in Bangladesh can instantly donate through our official bKash Merchant account, Nagad, Rocket, or direct bank transfer to our foundation's registered account at City Bank or BRAC Bank. Visit our Donation Page, select your preferred amount, and follow the simple mobile wallet instructions for instant confirmation.

Can international donors from the USA, UK, and worldwide donate online?

Yes. International supporters can donate using any major debit or credit card (Visa, MasterCard, American Express) through our 256-bit SSL encrypted global gateway. Contributions are automatically converted into local currency to fund relief projects without international friction.

Where are the foundation's clean water tube-wells and solar filtration plants located?

Our clean water initiatives prioritize arsenic-heavy and high-salinity zones in coastal Satkhira, Khulna, and drought-prone northern upazilas such as Kurigram and Gaibandha. Each deep tube-well and community solar filtration station delivers safe, laboratory-tested drinking water to approximately 250 to 500 villagers daily.

How does the Winter Warmth and Emergency Food Relief campaign operate?

During severe cold waves in northern Bangladesh, our teams distribute heavy-grade thermal blankets and warm winter clothing directly to destitute families, street children, and elderly individuals. During seasonal river flooding, we dispatch dry rations (rice, lentils, oil, oral rehydration salts, water purification tablets) by boat to stranded char communities.

What educational assistance and scholarships are provided to orphans?

The Mashiur Rahman Memorial Scholarship covers full school tuition, uniforms, textbooks, stationery, and daily nutritious meals for orphaned and underprivileged children. We also assign dedicated academic mentors who monitor attendance and personal growth, ensuring each student completes secondary schooling.

Are donations tax-exempt and will I receive a formal receipt?

Yes. The Mashiur Rahman Memorial Foundation is officially registered as a non-profit humanitarian trust. An automated digital tax receipt with registration and transaction numbers is sent to your registered email immediately following every successful online donation.

How can I become a volunteer or participate in on-the-ground relief missions?

We welcome passionate students, young professionals, and community leaders. Volunteers assist in organizing distribution drives, logistics, medical camps, and storytelling. Please fill out our brief form on the Contact Us page or email us with the subject line "Volunteer Application".

Can corporations, businesses, or educational institutions partner with you?

Absolutely. We collaborate with corporate social responsibility (CSR) departments, universities, and partner NGOs on targeted infrastructure projects such as school sanitation blocks, community solar pumps, and disaster rehabilitation. Please contact our leadership team at info@mashiurrahmanfoundation.org for institutional proposals.
